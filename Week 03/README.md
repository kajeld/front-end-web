# Adding CSS to Website
[Version One](https://editor.p5js.org/kajeld/full/OX9L3Bj2Y) My first version I wanted to give off an older film aesthetic. I choose black, red, and white as the primary colors because it reminded me of a darkroom and the colors you primarily see there. I also chose a font that was more analog that I thought is also associated with an older time. 
[Version Two](https://editor.p5js.org/kajeld/full/ZeBsazWtx) For my second version I wanted it to look more clean and modern. I was almost thinking of it like an editorial website or portfolio in a way. That's why I picked a lighter background and cleaner sans serif text. I also didn't align the grid totally with the gallery and film stock sections to add a more dynamic layer and movement.
# Website with Strong Visual Voice
I thought the PBS Kids website had a strong visual voice. It feels playful and energetic but the information is still organized and easy to understand. I also really enjoyed the games tab specifically. It make sense with their audience being younger children to have both words and strong imagery, but they do it in a way that it's enjoyable to whoever is on the page.
