## Midterm Progress

I think the hardest part for me was picking an idea. I find it hard to wrap my head around the fact that websites can be used as another medium for storytelling because, in my head, I tend to put them into a box of information and games, with a certain purpose and layout in mind. I started brainstorming things I like and themes I often make projects around, and since I really enjoy music, I began looking at different musicians' websites. I started to realize that a lot of them are pretty basic or formulaic compared to others in their industry or genre, and how quickly they can start to feel repetitive. I also think a lot of musicians' websites don't fully represent the musical world they've created or even the visual world they've built through their tours and other projects. I recently went to a Geese concert, so I checked out their website, which I liked and didn't feel like I would change much about. Then I went to Cameron Winter's website, and that was another story. It's super plain, and I think it barely scratches the surface of the visual world he's created around his album, especially with the new film and recording of the album at Carnegie Hall. I ended up deciding to remake his website, with the goal of reflecting the music and the visual world it evokes for me.
