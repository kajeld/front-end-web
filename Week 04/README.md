## Interesting Website 
[Zauberberg Productions](https://zauberbergproductions.com/)

I like the layout of this website because the names of the directors are centered on the page, and when you hover over a name, a moving clip related to their work plays in the background. I also think the half-page that opens when you click on someone’s name is really clever because you can still see the moving clip in the background, but there is enough contrast and distinction that the director is still highlighted. I like that the information doesn’t take up the entire screen or feel like a huge description, but is still enough to give you a sense of who they are and what they have worked on. I especially love the scrolling layout of their work at the bottom of the page because it lets you see a lot of their work without making the page feel crowded.
